Why this exists

Material science is one of the densest IP spaces there is. Thousands of overlapping filings, ambiguous claim language, chemistry families that span a dozen subfields. Understanding where you have freedom to operate normally means one of two things:

Option A — Consultant

A specialist who charges €300–600/h and takes two to four weeks to produce a landscape report.

Option B — Enterprise tool

PatSnap, Questel, Derwent — $15–40k/year for a team subscription. AI analysis costs extra.

Most researchers and early-stage teams have neither. So we built something that follows FTO best practice — and uses an LLM to read every relevant patent — quickly, locally, cheaply. Iterate in hours. Inform researchers early. Reduce consultant spend to the decisions that actually need a lawyer.

Compute

Local LLM (Qwen / Llama class) on a university GPU. No per-query cloud cost. No data leaves your network.

The LLM analysis layer

Each patent is sent to a local model with a custom persona prompt — the analytical lens that defines what question you are asking.

Persona examples

FTO analyst

Can we operate freely in this claim space, and if not, why?

Patentability examiner

Is there novelty here relative to the prior art?

Licensing officer

Which patents in this landscape are worth licensing, and at what leverage?

Output per patent

H M L Risk tier

+ Plain-language summary of what the patent covers

+ Claim-level reasoning

The market is moving this way

Several well-funded companies are pursuing AI-assisted patent work. They validate the approach — and define the gap this platform fills.

Patlytics · $65M raised

AI patent drafting, FTO, invalidity, claim charts. Used by 40%+ of Am Law 100 firms and corporates (Rivian, Canon, Xerox). Claims 80% project time reduction.

Target: law firms, large corporate IP. Price: enterprise (quoted).

GenIP · Listed (AIM)

Invention Evaluator — AI-assisted commercial viability reports for TTOs and research institutions. 7,000+ reports to 300+ universities. Premium tier (Invention Validator): >$12k/report.

Target: TTOs, universities. Addresses: "is this worth commercialising?" — not FTO.

IPRally · $13M raised

Graph-based AI patent search. Used by Nvidia, Dolby, Bentley. Strong on patentability and invalidity. Cloud-hosted, enterprise pricing.

Target: corporate IP teams. Price: enterprise.

All three are cloud-hosted, enterprise-priced, and aimed at law firms or large corporates. The open, self-hosted, research-institution end of the market is not served. That is the gap this platform addresses.
