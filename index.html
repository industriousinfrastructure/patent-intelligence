The problem

Material science is one of the densest patent spaces there is. Thousands of overlapping filings, ambiguous claim language, chemistry families spanning multiple subfields and jurisdictions. Getting a clear picture of where you can operate — or where to file — normally means one of two things:

Specialist consultant

€300–600/h. Two to four weeks for a landscape report. Expensive, slow, and the output often isn't reusable.

Enterprise platform

PatSnap, Questel, Derwent — $15–40k/year. AI analysis is an add-on. Designed for large corporate IP teams, not research institutions.

Most researchers, TTOs, and early-stage spinouts have neither the budget nor the time. Decisions get made without a proper landscape view — or the question doesn't get asked at all.
